Not your stereotypical matcha

Coffee is for hustle. Tea is for calm. Matcha never fit either. Real matcha is a paradox — calming yet energizing, grounding yet sharpening. It doesn't spike. It doesn't crash. It stays with you.

Region

First-harvest leaves sourced from Kyoto and Kagoshima, Japan.

Grade

Ceremonial-grade, crafted from carefully selected leaves for a smooth, refined cup.

Tasting Notes

Vibrant green, rich umami, fresh aroma. Full-bodied, calm, and gently energizing.
