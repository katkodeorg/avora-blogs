About Avora Matcha: Our Founders' Note

Avora Matcha was founded by Shruti and Elishia on one simple truth: matcha is for everyone. Ceremonial-grade matcha from first-harvest leaves in Kyoto and Kagoshima, Japan.

For many, matcha feels like a moment — a trend that travelled from Japan alongside Studio Ghibli films, anime, and a wave of cultural fascination. Beautiful, yes. But fleeting.

For Shruti, matcha was never a trend. It was transformational.

Shruti moves through life with the energy of a sprinter and the stamina of a marathon runner — driven by an internal fire and fuelled by big dreams. A certified yoga instructor, a software engineer, a model, and the founder of Sahai (a non-profit mental health organisation), she needed something that could keep up with her pace without slowing her down.

Coffee made her jittery, followed by an inevitable crash. Tea was calming, but too gentle. And then came matcha. It met her exactly where she was: a steady rise, sustained energy, focus without frenzy, power without burnout. Cup after cup, it supported her active lifestyle, sharpened her mind, and grounded her body. Once it became part of her rhythm, it stayed.

Matcha deserved a place in everyday life

Shruti saw matcha not just as a drink, but as a quiet ally — one that could support a CEO powering through long days, a dancer moving through rehearsals, or anyone who cares about their health, energy, and inner balance. She believed matcha deserved a place in everyday life, without elitism and without stereotypes. That belief became Avora Matcha.

Avora truly came alive when Shruti met a kindred spirit — Elishia. A marketer by day, a devoted matcha lover by heart, and an unwavering believer in Shruti's vision, Elishia joined as a partner on the journey.

Matcha is for everyone

Together, they built Avora on one simple truth: matcha is for everyone. No labels. No archetypes. Just the best quality matcha, meant to help you feel your best, live fully, and show up as yourself — whatever that looks like.

We're so excited for you to try Avora — just really good matcha that gives you a high that lasts.

With love & matcha,
Shruti & Elishia
