How to Make a Matcha Latte (Hot or Iced)

A simple matcha latte recipe you can make at home in five minutes. The right ratio of matcha, water, and milk for a smooth, cafe-quality latte, hot or iced.

A matcha latte is the easiest way to enjoy matcha every day, and it is far cheaper and better than the sugary cafe version. Once you have the ratio down, it takes about five minutes. Here is how to make it, hot or iced.

What you need

  • 1 to 2 teaspoons of matcha (about 2g). A good ceremonial matcha like Avora makes a noticeably smoother, sweeter latte.
  • A splash of hot water (about 60ml, below boiling, 70 to 80°C)
  • 150 to 200ml of milk (dairy or your favorite plant milk)
  • Optional: honey, maple, or a little sugar to taste
  • A bamboo whisk or a small electric frother

Hot matcha latte

  1. Sift 1 to 2 teaspoons of matcha into a mug or bowl to remove clumps.
  2. Add a splash of hot water (not boiling) and whisk briskly in a “W” motion for about 30 seconds until smooth and slightly frothy. This is your matcha concentrate.
  3. Heat and froth your milk, then pour it over the concentrate.
  4. Sweeten if you like, stir, and enjoy.

Iced matcha latte

  1. Sift and whisk the matcha with a splash of hot water, exactly as above, to get a smooth concentrate with no clumps.
  2. Fill a glass with ice and pour in your cold milk.
  3. Pour the matcha concentrate over the top for that classic layered look, then stir before drinking.

Whisking with a little hot water first is the secret even for an iced latte: it dissolves the matcha smoothly so you never get a gritty, clumpy drink.

Tips for the best matcha latte

  • Do not use boiling water. It scorches the matcha and turns it bitter. See why matcha tastes like grass if yours does.
  • Sift every time. Clumps are the number one reason home lattes come out lumpy.
  • Start with less sweetener than a cafe. Good matcha is naturally a little sweet, so you need less than you think.
  • Quality shows through milk. Cheap matcha tastes grassy even in a latte. A first-harvest ceremonial matcha stays smooth. This is where Avora shines.

Which milk is best?

Any milk works. Whole dairy milk is the creamiest. Oat milk is the most popular plant option because it froths well and is naturally sweet. Almond and soy also work nicely. Try a few and pick your favorite.
